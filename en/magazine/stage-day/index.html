STAGE DAY · TOKYO / YOKOHAMA

Make the performance the main event

Seven theatres for ballet, musicals, kabuki, theatre and classical music — with room for the time before and after.

Updated Sep 12, 2026Kibun editorial

A performance does not need to be “just the evening plan.” Start with the stage, then let the rest of the day stay light.

01
Shibuya · Tokyo

New National Theatre Tokyo

Japan’s national venue for opera, ballet, dance and theatre in Hatsudai. Choose the performance first and build the rest of the day around it.

# CHANGELOG v0.8

## Data
- Expanded spot database from 40 to 55.
- Added 15 production-reviewed Kanagawa spots focused on nature, water, animals, culture and parent enjoyment.
- Added public copy, vibe scores, experience scores, dynamic snapshots and Buzz metadata for all 15.

## Availability
- Added optional `availability_constraints.unavailable_until`.
- Recommender now excludes temporarily unavailable spots automatically.
- Yokosuka Museum of Art is excluded through 2026-09-04 and becomes eligible from 2026-09-05.

## Images
- Added licensed real-photo heroes for Arasaki Park, Hakone Open-Air Museum and Enoshima Aquarium.
- Other new spots use clearly labeled AI mood images until a stronger licensed/approved real photo is available.

## Quality
- User-facing `public_copy` remains separated from internal `editorial_reason`.

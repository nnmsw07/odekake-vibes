# v20.11.13

SNS screenshot no-photo cards were redesigned after v20.11.12 looked too much like a dashboard/UI component.

- Removed QUICK NOTE / MOOD CHECK / BEST FOR / KIBUN MOMENT labels.
- Removed duplicated oversized slide numbers inside the card.
- Removed nested mini-card grids and pill-heavy layouts.
- Replaced five gimmicky layouts with three quieter editorial treatments:
  - statement: one strong Japanese editorial line
  - reasons: three simple reasons with rules, no boxes
  - moment: dark editorial quote card
- Reduced no-photo lead copy to avoid clipping and text overload.
- Existing rights-safe real photos still take priority over note cards.
- AI theme cover on slide 1 is unchanged.
